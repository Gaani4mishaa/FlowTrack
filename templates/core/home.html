How it works

A report moves through a clear process

From the first observation to the latest update, each step is recorded in FlowTrack.

  1. 1

    Report an issue

    Describe the problem and location. Add a photo or coordinates if available.

  2. 2

    Review and assign

    The report is assigned to an organization for review and prioritization.

  3. 3

    Maintenance work

    A manager coordinates work by assigning an engineer to an eligible report.

  4. 4

    Track progress

    Residents can sign in to see the status updates recorded for their reports.

What can be reported

Drainage problems we track

Choose the issue type that best describes what you observe.

Blocked drain

Leaves or debris restrict water flow.

Flooding

Water collects on streets or nearby areas.

Damaged culvert

A culvert or crossing is damaged.

Overflowing drain

A drain cannot contain or carry the water.

Waste obstructing drainage

Waste is blocking a drainage route.

Damaged drainage channel

A channel is broken or visibly deteriorated.

Other drainage issue

Report another drainage concern.
